# Jarem Norwood | Finance & Data Analytics Portfolio

A personal website showcasing projects at the intersection of finance, data analysis, and practical problem-solving.

This portfolio brings together work developed through coursework and independent learning. It is a place to document not only the results of each project, but also the questions behind it, the methods used, and the limitations of the analysis.

## About the Website

I created this website to explore topics that interest me, apply what I am learning, and build a record of my work over time. My longer-term career interests include investment banking, private wealth management, and corporate law. The projects here help me develop relevant skills while learning more about these different fields.

The site includes:

- **Projects:** Analyses and research with explanations of the questions, methods, and findings.
- **About:** My interests, motivations, and approach to learning through projects.
- **Resume:** An overview of my education, experience, and skills.
- **Contact:** A way to get in touch.

## Featured Project

### Portfolio Risk, Return & Financial Forecasting

My featured project examines portfolio risk and return across **12 U.S. industries** and compares approaches to financial time-series forecasting.

The project focuses on interpreting the results of financial analysis, understanding the assumptions behind different methods, and communicating what the findings can—and cannot—tell us.

For the full analysis, see the featured project on the website.

## How I Approach Projects

My process varies by topic, but generally centers on three stages:

1. **Research:** Identify a question, understand the relevant financial concepts, and find appropriate data.
2. **Application:** Apply and test methods using the tools and techniques I am learning.
3. **Evaluation:** Interpret the results, examine limitations, and reflect on what I would improve.

## Ongoing Development
